Experiment 1: Performance Analysis of Erbium-Doped Fiber Amplifier (EDFA)    

### About the Experiment 
The aim of this experiment is to study and analyze the performance of an Erbium-Doped Fiber Amplifier (EDFA), an important optical amplifier used in fiber-optic communication systems. The experiment focuses on understanding the working principle of EDFA, its gain characteristics, output power, and amplification performance. It also helps students understand the role of optical amplifiers in compensating for signal losses during long-distance optical communication.
